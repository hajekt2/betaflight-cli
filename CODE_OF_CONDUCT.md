# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and technically honest.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Keep safety discussions factual and treat flight-control risks seriously.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and focused on protecting the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method listed on the [repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/hajekt2).
Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, release discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
